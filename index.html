Support & Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Atelier Duo on iPhone Duo and iOS devices.

Why is Atelier Duo designed specifically for iPhone Duo?

iPhone Duo represents a paradigm shift in mobile form factors. Rather than treating foldable screens as a single stretched phone, Atelier Duo treats the twin OLED panels as genuine physical wallet wings—giving you authentic leathercraft permanency, real-time hinge physics, and zero layout flicker.

Can I use Atelier Duo on standard single-screen iPhones?

Yes! While engineered primarily for iPhone Duo, the app features an adaptive companion mode for standard iPhones: a refined portrait showcase on the cover, and an automatic expansion into the 10-slot bifold upon rotating into landscape orientation.

Is my financial data, card metadata, or note content safe?

Completely safe. Atelier Duo is an offline-first application. We operate zero remote servers, collect zero personal telemetry, and store everything locally within your iPhone’s sandboxed encrypted storage protected by Face ID and Apple Secure Enclave.

What does the Bespoke Pass In-App Purchase include?

The Atelier Duo Bespoke Pass is a one-time lifetime non-consumable buyout ($1.99 USD). It unlocks all 10 card slots, the smart statement credit ledger, exclusive artisan leather patinas, and mechanical watch complications with zero recurring subscriptions.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open Atelier Duo, tap the Bespoke Crown or visit Settings, and tap Restore Purchases. Your purchase will be verified instantly via Apple StoreKit 2 at no charge.
