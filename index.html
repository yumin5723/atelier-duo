Support & Help

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers regarding features, compatibility, and purchase management.

Does Atelier Duo require an active internet connection?

No. Atelier Duo is intentionally engineered as an offline-first luxury utility. Your cards, slot arrangements, and notes never leave your iPhone and are protected directly by iOS hardware encryption.

How does the dual-screen foldable experience work?

On dual-screen foldable devices (such as iPhone Duo), the app automatically coordinates across both displays with 0-gap physical stitch alignment. On standard single-screen iPhones, the app provides an adaptive showcase in portrait and a landscape bifold mode.

What does the Bespoke Pass In-App Purchase include?

The Atelier Duo Bespoke Pass is a one-time lifetime non-consumable buyout. It permanently unlocks all 10 card slots, artisan leather patinas (Bordeaux, Hermes, Iridescent), mechanical watch complication dials, and all future updates with no recurring subscriptions.

How do I restore my previous purchase on a new device?

Open Atelier Duo on your new iPhone with the same Apple ID. Tap the Bespoke Crown or visit Settings, then tap Restore Purchases. Your lifetime Bespoke Pass will be validated immediately via Apple StoreKit 2 without any charge.
