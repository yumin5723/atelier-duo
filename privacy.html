Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 3, 2026 • Last Updated: October 2026

Summary: Atelier Duo: Luxury Bifold is an offline-first luxury utility. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or monetize any of your personal information, card metadata, notes, or biometric data. Your privacy belongs entirely to you.

1. Introduction

Atelier Duo ("we," "our," or "the App") is developed and maintained by independent developer Wanglei Liu. We are dedicated to delivering a premier digital luxury leathercraft and horology experience while holding user privacy and data security as our highest principle.

This Privacy Policy outlines how your data is handled when you use Atelier Duo: Luxury Bifold on iOS devices.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

Unlike many modern apps, Atelier Duo does not maintain remote servers or databases. Specifically, we do NOT collect:

  • Personal Identifiable Information (PII): No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses are collected.
  • Financial & Payment Card Data: The cards you view or configure in the wallet are stored exclusively on your local device. We never collect or transmit credit card numbers, CVVs, expiration dates, or bank details.
  • Location Data: The App does not request, track, or access your GPS or network location.
  • Biometric Data: Any biometric authentication (Face ID or Touch ID) is executed strictly on-device through Apple LocalAuthentication APIs and never shared with us.
  • Analytics and Tracking: We do not integrate third-party tracking libraries, advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network), or user behavior analytic services.

3. On-Device Storage & Data Security

All user-generated content—such as slot assignments, personalized card names, custom leather choices, and notes—is saved exclusively within your iOS device’s local sandbox storage using standard Apple Foundation and Data Protection encryption standards.

Your data is protected by your device passcode and Apple Secure Enclave. We have no mechanism to remotely access, read, or recover your local wallet data.

4. In-App Purchases (StoreKit 2)

Atelier Duo offers an optional one-time In-App Purchase known as the Atelier Duo Bespoke Pass.

All purchase transactions are handled exclusively by Apple Inc. through the official Apple App Store StoreKit 2 framework. Apple acts as the merchant of record and processes your payment details in accordance with Apple’s Privacy Policy. We receive only anonymized transaction tokens to verify your entitlement status and unlock premium features locally. We never receive or store your billing address, credit card numbers, or banking credentials.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all data is stored exclusively on your device, you have complete and total control over your data:

  • You can modify, reorganize, or remove any card slot directly from the app interface at any time.
  • Deleting the Atelier Duo app from your iOS device will instantly and permanently erase all associated local data and cache files from your device storage.

6. Children’s Privacy

Atelier Duo does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). Given that our application collects zero personal data from any user, it fully complies with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

7. Third-Party Links

Our support website may contain links to external developer resources (such as Apple's official documentation). We do not control and are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party websites.

8. International Compliance (GDPR & CCPA)

For users in the European Union (GDPR) and California (CCPA/CPRA): Since Atelier Duo does not process, store, or transfer personal data to remote servers, your rights to access, rectify, port, or erase personal data are naturally fulfilled through on-device management and local application deletion.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or applicable legal regulations. Any revisions will be published directly on this webpage with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp. We encourage you to review this policy occasionally.
